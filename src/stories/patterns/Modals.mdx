# Modals

This section documents reusable modal patterns built from `ModalFrame`.

## Danger modal

Use a danger modal when the action is destructive, irreversible, and important enough to require an explicit confirmation step.

<Canvas of={Stories.DangerModal} sourceState="hidden" />

## When to use

- Use a danger modal for deletion, archival, or other irreversible actions.
- Keep the message direct about what will be removed and what cannot be recovered.
- Pair the destructive action with a clearly labeled cancel path.
